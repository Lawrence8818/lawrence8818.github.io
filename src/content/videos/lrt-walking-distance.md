## Key takeaways

- “Near the LRT” comes in two kinds: walkable, and reachable only by car.
- Five minutes on foot and five minutes by car are completely different things.
- Distance on a map is often misleading: there may be a main road to cross, and in the rain you cannot walk it at all.
- If you will take the LRT every day, walk the actual route on site before you book.

## What I say in the video

A friend of mine bought a condo next to the train line a few years ago. When we talked recently, it turned out he still has to drive to the LRT station. Why? He said walking there takes 20 minutes, there is a main road to cross in between, and on rainy days it simply cannot be walked. He laughed at himself: he thought he had bought a condo beside the station, when it was only fairly near the station.

Stations really come in two kinds: those you can walk to, and those you can only reach by car. Five minutes is five minutes, but five minutes on foot and five minutes by car are completely different things.

What the map shows is often not very accurate. Especially if you need that LRT station every day, I will take you to the site to see for yourself how far the walk from the condo to the station really is. Or you can use an app to verify it.

Doing one more site visit beforehand is so that when you collect your keys, it is the same as what you saw.
