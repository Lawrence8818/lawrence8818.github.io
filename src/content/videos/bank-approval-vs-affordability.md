## Key takeaways

- A bank approving the loan does not mean you can comfortably afford the home.
- Once the instalment passes half of household income, daily life gets very tight.
- A safer guide is to keep the instalment at around one third of household income.
- On a household income of RM 9,000, that points to an instalment of about RM 3,000.

## What I say in the video

A couple sat in front of me. Their household income was about RM 9,000 a month and they had their eye on a home with an instalment of RM 4,000. The wife loved it. The husband said nothing.

So I asked: with the RM 5,000 that is left, you still have the car loan, the children, insurance and utilities. If something unexpected comes up and you need cash, is it enough?

I did not tell them to buy or not to buy. I only laid the numbers out on the table. Once the instalment is more than half of household income, life becomes a real strain.

As a general guide, keep the instalment at about one third of household income, and at most not more than half, so that you can sleep at night. In other words, on RM 9,000 you are looking for a home with an instalment of around RM 3,000, which over a 35-year loan is roughly a RM 700,000 home.

They did not sign that day. But before leaving, the husband said: “Finally someone has worked the numbers out for us.”

What they wanted was never simply a bigger unit. It was a decision that suited them, and one they could sleep soundly with.
