## Key takeaways

- The same budget in two different locations gives two very different daily lives.
- What buyers overlook most is what they can reach on foot.
- Convenience cannot be felt in a show unit; by the time you move in, it is too late.
- At your next viewing, walk out and see which daily needs you can meet within 10 minutes on foot.

## What I say in the video

Two families with about the same budget buy in two different locations, and the experience is completely different.

The first family has a coffee shop, a bank and a pharmacy downstairs. Anything they need is a five-minute walk. On weekends they can go down for breakfast.

The second family has nothing around them. Even buying a packet of salt is a four-minute drive. Much of the time, half their weekend is spent driving to wherever they need to go: groceries, daily necessities, taking the children out.

The difference is not really in the property itself. What many people overlook when viewing is walking: where can you get to on foot? Convenience is something you cannot feel in a show unit, and by the time you feel it after moving in, it is too late.

Next time you view a home, look at the unit, then walk out and see where you can get to within 10 minutes on foot and which daily needs that covers. You are not only buying a house. You are buying the rhythm of every day that follows.
