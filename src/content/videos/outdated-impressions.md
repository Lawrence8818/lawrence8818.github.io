## Key takeaways

- Many people’s impression of an area is stuck several years in the past.
- Example: after the roads in Bukit Jalil were changed, a long-time resident told me traffic in and out now flows smoothly both ways.
- Without updated information, some people rule out a place that would actually suit them.
- Whichever area you are looking at, set the old impression aside, check what it is like now, then decide.

## What I say in the video

The other day at Pavilion Bukit Jalil I met a buyer who lives in the area. He told me something that overturns what many people believe about the traffic there.

There had been a news report saying the roads there were changed and the jams would get worse. Many people who saw it quietly crossed the area off their list.

But this buyer has lived there for many years and still does. He told me that before the roads were changed it was very congested, every single day. Since the change, getting in and out is smooth in both directions. He told me he hopes they never change the roads back.

I thought about it for a long time afterwards. Many people believe it is jammed because they are still living in the old picture. The roads changed and the jam eased, but they do not know, because they never updated what they know. So they still think it is jammed, and miss a place that would actually suit them well.

Whichever area you are looking at, put the old impression aside first. Find out whether things today are still the way you remember, and then decide. It will not be too late.

Many opportunities are not ones you never came across. They were simply kept outside the door by an old impression.
