KLCC is the best-known address in Malaysia. For this page I group the city-centre projects together: those around the Twin Towers, and those in the neighbouring Bukit Bintang, TRX and Jalan Ampang areas, since buyers usually compare them against one another.

## Why buyers look at KLCC

- **The address itself**: the Twin Towers, Suria KLCC and KLCC Park are landmarks every tenant and buyer already knows.
- **Rail in several directions**: the Kelana Jaya LRT line stops at KLCC, and MRT stations serve Bukit Bintang and TRX.
- **A choice of new and completed**: some projects here are already built, so you can walk through the actual building rather than a show unit.

## How to choose between the projects

1. **Decide the purpose first.** Own stay and rental point to different buildings and different unit sizes.
2. **Then the monthly instalment.** The table above is sorted from the lowest. Several projects do not have a published estimate yet; ask me for those.
3. **Then the size.** City-centre units run from very compact suites to large residences, and the table shows the range for each.
4. **Then tenure and completion.** Freehold and leasehold are both common here, and completed buildings sit alongside ones still under construction.

## What to watch for

- **Residential or commercial title.** It changes your utility rates and yearly charges.
- **Maintenance fees.** Hotel-style facilities cost more to run. Each project page lists the rate.
- **Short-term rental rules.** Not every building allows it. Check before you buy for that purpose.
- **The view.** A Twin Towers view today can be blocked by a future tower. I check what is planned next door.

Availability and packages change month to month. Tell me your budget and purpose and I will tell you honestly which of these fit.
