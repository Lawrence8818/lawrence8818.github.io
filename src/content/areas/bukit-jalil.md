Bukit Jalil has grown from a stadium district into one of the most complete townships in Kuala Lumpur. Daily life here does not depend on driving into the city: the mall, the park, schools and the LRT are all within the township itself.

## Why buyers look at Bukit Jalil

- **Everything is already there**: Pavilion Bukit Jalil for shopping and dining, the 80-acre Bukit Jalil Recreational Park for weekends, and the National Stadium and sports complex next door.
- **Rail access**: the Sri Petaling LRT line runs through the area. Some projects are within walking distance of a station; Queenswoodz, for example, is about 480 m from LRT Awan Besar.
- **Family-sized homes**: many of the launches here are three bedrooms or larger, which is why upgraders shortlist this area.

## How to choose between the projects

1. **Start with the monthly instalment.** The table above is sorted from the lowest instalment upward. Decide what you are comfortable paying each month before you look at anything else.
2. **Then match the size to your household.** A couple and a family of five need very different layouts. The size range in the table tells you quickly which projects are even in the running.
3. **Then look at what is at the doorstep.** The projects differ here. Park Green is about 350 m from the recreational park and has a pedestrian bridge to Pavilion Bukit Jalil. Vividz sits in KL Wellness City, opposite Pavilion Bukit Jalil. OAKA Residences is next to the golf course and park, and is pet-friendly.
4. **Finally, check tenure and completion.** Freehold or leasehold, and how long you are willing to wait for keys.

## What to watch for

- **Walkable is not the same as near.** A station can be close on the map and still be an unpleasant walk. I check the actual route on foot.
- **Density and car parks.** Units per floor, lifts per tower and the number of car parks per unit vary a lot between projects.
- **Maintenance fees.** They differ from project to project and are a monthly cost on top of your instalment. Each project page lists the rate.

Availability and packages change month to month. Tell me your budget and household size and I will tell you honestly which of these fit, and which do not.
