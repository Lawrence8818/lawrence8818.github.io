Bangsar has been one of Kuala Lumpur's most sought-after neighbourhoods for decades. Bangsar South, next door, is the newer hub of offices and high-rise homes. Buyers often consider both, so I have put them on one page, and marked clearly which project is in which.

## Why buyers look at Bangsar

- **An established neighbourhood**: restaurants, cafes, shops and schools that have been there for years.
- **Between the city and PJ**: on the Kelana Jaya LRT line, with KL Sentral in one direction and Petaling Jaya in the other.
- **New and completed side by side**: several Bangsar South buildings are already completed, so you can see the real thing.

## How to choose between the projects

1. **Bangsar or Bangsar South.** Decide which of the two suits your daily life. The table shows the location under each project name.
2. **Then the monthly instalment.** The table is sorted from the lowest. Several projects do not have a published estimate yet; ask me for those.
3. **Then the size.** The range runs from compact units to very large residences.
4. **Then tenure and completion.** Most are leasehold, a few are freehold, and some are ready now.

## What to watch for

- **Homes and offices are both on this list.** Some Bangsar South buildings are office suites, not residences. Open the project page to see which.
- **Traffic at peak hours.** Popular areas are busy ones. I will tell you what the drive is really like at the times you travel.
- **Maintenance fees.** They differ from project to project. Each project page lists the rate.

Availability and packages change month to month. Tell me your budget and how you plan to use the unit, and I will tell you honestly which of these fit.
