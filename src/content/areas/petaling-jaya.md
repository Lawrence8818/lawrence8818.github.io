Petaling Jaya is where the Klang Valley grew up. What started as KL's first satellite city is now a full city in its own right, with jobs, education, healthcare and retail all inside its own borders, and a resident base that spans three generations.

## Why Petaling Jaya holds its value

- **Complete amenities**: established schools, medical centres such as those in the SS2 and Damansara corridors, and malls from Paradigm to 1 Utama on its doorstep;
- **Real transport options**: the Kelana Jaya LRT line runs through PJ, and the LDP, Federal Highway and NPE connect it to KL, Subang and beyond;
- **Scarce land, proven demand**: PJ has very little empty land left, so new projects are infill developments in neighbourhoods where demand is already proven.

## How I help you choose a property in PJ

1. First talk through budget, commute and family needs, and narrow down to the PJ segments that suit you, from SS2 and Seksyen 17 to Kelana Jaya and Bandar Utama fringe;

## What to watch for in PJ

- **Older stock vs new launches**: PJ has plenty of subsale options; I help you compare a new launch honestly against nearby subsale value before you commit;
- **Density and parking**: infill projects vary widely in units per floor and visitor parking, details that matter more in a mature area;
- **Leasehold pockets**: parts of PJ are leasehold, so always check the remaining tenure before falling in love with a price.
