Kwasa Damansara is one of the largest new townships in the Klang Valley, planned around its MRT stations. Because it is still being built, the projects here differ more than usual in what is already at the doorstep, so comparing them side by side matters.

## Why buyers look at Kwasa Damansara

- **MRT at the centre of the plan**: the township has stations at Kwasa Damansara and Kwasa Sentral. D'Evia, for example, is about 600 m from Kwasa Sentral station.
- **A new township next to established ones**: Kota Damansara and its amenities are close by while Kwasa Damansara's own are being built.
- **A wide range**: from compact units for first-time buyers to large family layouts.

## How to choose between the projects

1. **Start with the monthly instalment.** The table above is sorted from the lowest. Some projects do not have a published estimate yet; ask me for those.
2. **Then match the size to your household.** The size range in the table shows quickly which projects are in the running.
3. **Then check the walk to the MRT.** In a rail-centred township this is the detail that changes daily life the most.
4. **Finally, tenure and completion.** Freehold and leasehold are both available, and completion dates range over several years.

## What to watch for

- **What is built now versus what is planned.** I will show you what already exists around each project and what is still on paper.
- **The actual walking route to the station.** Close on the map is not always a comfortable walk.
- **Maintenance fees.** They differ from project to project. Each project page lists the rate.

Availability and packages change month to month. Tell me your budget and household size and I will tell you honestly which of these fit.
